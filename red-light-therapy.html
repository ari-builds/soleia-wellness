What it is

Light that works with your body

Red light therapy uses low-level red and near-infrared light on your skin. Your cells absorb the light and use it to support your skin, muscles and joints.

  • 10 to 20 minute sessions in a warm, restful room
  • No recovery time needed. You can carry on with your day
  • Works alongside your training, work and daily routine

What to expect

Your first session

  1. 1 Check in, take a seat and pick your session length.
  2. 2 Settle in under the panels in the warm, low-light room.
  3. 3 Rest for 10 to 20 minutes while the light does the work.
  4. 4 Get up, rehydrate, and get back to your day.
