What it is

Breathe in, slow down

At the oxygen bar you sit back, pick a flavored or plain option, and breathe concentrated oxygen through a nasal tube. The air feels fresh, the room is calm, and you stay as long as you like. Hyperbaric sessions in the chamber take it a step further under gentle pressure.

  • Oxygen sessions at the bar, at your own pace
  • Hyperbaric oxygen chamber sessions available
  • A lounging-style reset that pairs well with any visit

What to expect

Your first session

  1. 1 Pick a seat at the bar and a flavor or a plain option.
  2. 2 Breathe normally through the nasal tube.
  3. 3 Sit back for 15 to 45 minutes. Start shorter if you like.
  4. 4 Return to your day with a clear head.
