What it is

Heat that reaches deep

An infrared sauna uses infrared heaters that warm your body directly instead of heating the air around you. The heat reaches deeper into your muscles, with milder air temperature you can actually breathe.

  • 20 to 40 minute sessions, timed to how you feel
  • Comfortable filters and a cool, fresh feel compared to steam
  • Step straight into the cold plunge after for the full circuit

What to expect

Your first session

  1. 1 Check in and change into comfortable, loose clothes.
  2. 2 Settle into the warm cabin and pick your heat level.
  3. 3 Sweat, breathe, and let your shoulders drop.
  4. 4 Cool down, drink water, and rehydrate after.
