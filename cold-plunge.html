What it is

Cold on purpose

A cold plunge is a short soak in cold, clean water, usually three to five minutes. The chill resets your mind, eases sore muscles, and leaves you alert and steady. You control the temperature and the timing.

  • 3 to 5 minute sessions. Short and done on purpose
  • Clean, filtered cold water maintained for you
  • Coaches available on site if you have never plunged

What to expect

Your first session

  1. 1 Breathe. Get comfortable with the water temperature.
  2. 2 Lower yourself in slowly and settle your breathing.
  3. 3 Stay for two to five minutes. Less is fine on day one.
  4. 4 Step out, dry off, and warm up with a sauna right after.
