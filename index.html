What we offer

Six therapies, one space, built for full-body recovery. Add a few to a single visit, or come in for one.

01

Red Light Therapy

Near-infrared and red wavelengths that work at the cellular level to support skin, muscle, and recovery. A quiet, warm session with visible benefits.

02

Infrared Sauna

Deep-reaching infrared heat that warms you from within. Sweat, reset, and unwind without the heavy, stale air of a traditional sauna.

03

Cold Plunge

A controlled cold plunge that sharpens your mind, soothes muscles, and triggers the body's natural recovery response. The hot-and-cold finish your sauna needs.

04

IV Hydration

Injectable hydration and vitamin blends delivered in a comfortable lounge chair. Restore energy, immunity, and balance in under an hour.

05

Oxygen Bar & Hyperbaric

Concentrated oxygen, breathed at your own pace. A light, restorative reset for focus, stress, and recovery. Hyperbaric sessions available.

06

Massage Therapy

Hands-on massage that releases tightness and brings the body back into balance. The finishing touch after a full recovery circuit.
